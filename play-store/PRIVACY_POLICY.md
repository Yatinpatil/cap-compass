# CAP Compass Privacy Policy

**Last updated: October 26, 2026**

CAP Compass is an independent MHT-CET CAP preference planner. This policy explains what information the app uses, where it is stored, and what happens when you choose to copy or share your list.

## Information you enter

If you use the planner, you may enter an optional name, MHT-CET percentile, category, gender selection for a quota estimate, home university, preferred branches and cities. The app also saves your course choices and checklist progress.

## How the app uses and stores information

CAP Compass uses these details only to calculate sample estimates, personalize the planner and checklist, and save your progress. The information is stored in local app storage on your device. The app has no account system or developer-operated server and does not send this information to CAP Compass. Automatic Android cloud backup and device-transfer backup are disabled for the app.

## Copying, sharing and external links

- When you tap Copy, the app places the preference list text on your device clipboard.
- When you tap Share or export a CSV, the app passes the selected list to Android's system share sheet. The receiving app or person you choose may store or handle it under their own terms. Export files are created temporarily in app cache.
- The official CET Cell link opens its website in an external browser. CAP Compass does not attach your planner profile to that link.

These actions are optional and happen only after you select them.

## Data retention and deletion

Your profile, course list and checklist remain in local app storage until you use “Reset plan” in the app or uninstall the app. Reset plan clears those saved planner records. It cannot remove text you previously copied or data already received by another app through sharing.

## Security

App data is kept within Android's app storage sandbox. CAP Compass does not add its own encryption layer, so protect your device with a screen lock and do not enter CET portal passwords or one-time codes into this planner.

## Children

CAP Compass is an admissions-planning tool and is not directed to children under 13.

## Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email the developer using the support contact listed on the CAP Compass Google Play listing. The Play listing must include an active support email before publication.

## Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, this policy will be updated before or with the relevant app release. The latest version will be available from the app and the public policy URL in the Play Store listing.
