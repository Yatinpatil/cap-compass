## Full description

Plan your Maharashtra engineering admission choices with CAP Compass.

Explore sample college and branch cut-offs, create a preference list in the order you would choose it, and track CAP application steps and documents in one place.

With CAP Compass, you can:

- Compare sample closing-percentile estimates for colleges and branches
- Filter by city, branch, college type and estimated fit
- Add specific college and branch choices to your CAP list
- Reorder choices and review the balance of your list
- Copy, share or export the list as a CSV file
- Track application tasks and a category-based document checklist
- Keep your profile and progress in local app storage on your device

CAP Compass does not submit an application or preference form for you. Cut-offs are sample planning estimates, not official CET Cell data or an admission guarantee. Always verify the current information brochure, deadlines, seat rules, institute codes, course codes and cut-offs on the official State CET Cell portal before submitting.

CAP Compass is an independent planner and is not affiliated with the State CET Cell.
